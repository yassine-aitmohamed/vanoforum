Resources

A curated list of portals, scholarships, classic essays, newsletters and tools for researchers. Every link was checked by hand. Suggest a new one in the forum.

Finding a PhD or a postdoc

  • EURAXESSThe European Commission portal for research jobs, funding and mobility in Europe, with practical help for researchers moving country.
  • FindAPhDA large search engine for doctoral projects, strongest for the United Kingdom and Europe.
  • jobs.ac.ukAcademic and research vacancies, including funded PhD studentships, mainly in the United Kingdom.
  • AcademicPositionsInternational listings of PhD, postdoc and faculty positions, with many European universities.
  • ADUMThe platform used by many French doctoral schools to publish thesis offers and manage applications.
  • MathJobsThe American Mathematical Society system for academic mathematics positions, mainly in the United States and Canada.

Scholarships and funding

  • DAADThe German Academic Exchange Service: scholarships and a searchable database of doctoral programmes in Germany.
  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie ActionsEuropean Union funding for doctoral networks and postdoctoral fellowships, open to all nationalities.
  • Mitacs GlobalinkResearch internships in Canada for undergraduates abroad, often a first step towards graduate study there.
  • Campus FranceThe official guide to studying in France, with a catalogue of doctoral schools and scholarship information.

Classic essays on research and writing

  • Career advice, by Terence TaoA long collection of short essays on how to work, learn, write and build a research career in mathematics.
  • You and your research, by Richard HammingA famous talk on why some scientists do important work and others do not: courage, open doors, and the important problems of your field.
  • How to write mathematics, by Paul HalmosThe classic short essay on mathematical exposition: say something, know your reader, write in spirals.
  • How to read a paper, by S. KeshavThe two-page note that introduced the three-pass method for reading research papers.

Newsletters on Substack

  • Research WritingVaranya Chaubey writes on how to structure and revise academic prose.
  • Research FreedomLennart Nacke writes on academic writing workflows, productivity and publishing, aimed at early-career researchers.
  • About academic writingFemke de Jong writes on the craft and habits of academic writing.
  • Write what you likeEssays on writing habits and the writing life of researchers.

Finding and organising papers

  • arXivThe open preprint server for mathematics, physics, computer science and neighbouring fields.
  • zbMATH OpenA free and open database of reviews of the mathematical literature since the nineteenth century.
  • Google ScholarBroad search with citation counts and the list of papers citing each work.
  • Semantic ScholarA free search engine with citation graphs, author pages and paper alerts.
  • Connected PapersA visual graph of papers related to one starting paper, useful for a literature review.
  • ZoteroA free, open-source reference manager that exports to BibTeX.

Choosing a trustworthy journal

  • Think. Check. Submit.A checklist to decide whether a journal or a publisher can be trusted.
  • DOAJThe Directory of Open Access Journals, a curated list of reputable open-access journals.
  • SCImago Journal RankJournal rankings and quartiles (Q1 to Q4) by subject area, based on Scopus data.
