The background is an exact solution of the Schrödinger equation: a superposition of eigenstates of the two-dimensional harmonic oscillator with frequency ratio √2, so the motion never repeats. Hue is the phase of ψ, brightness is |ψ|², the faint curves are level sets of |ψ|², and the particles follow Bohmian trajectories, dx/dt = Im(∇ψ/ψ).
