# Code of conduct

VanoForum is a place to learn how research works. Many readers are students writing in a second language, asking questions they could not ask elsewhere.

**Be precise.** Answer the question that was asked. Give sources for rules, deadlines and programmes.

**Be kind.** No question is too basic. Correct mistakes in the text, never the person. Criticise work, ideas and arguments, never people.

**Credit others.** Cite the guides, papers and people you build on. Do not post work that is not yours.

**Keep it safe.** No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam or advertising. Do not share other people's private emails, reviews or documents.

Posts that break these rules are edited or removed. Repeated or serious violations lead to a ban from the forum. Report a problem by opening a discussion in Q&A marked "moderation", or by writing to the maintainer.
