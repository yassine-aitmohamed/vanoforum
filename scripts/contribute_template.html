Contribute to VanoForum

Write your text in Word or LaTeX, as you usually do. The site turns it into a page with the layout, fonts and formulas of the other guides, and your name is shown on what you wrote. The editors review every contribution before it goes online.

Before you write

  • Check what already exists. Search the guides first. If your topic is already covered, add a section to that guide instead of writing a new one, so that readers find everything in one place.
  • Bring something new: the rules of your country or your field, a worked example, a mistake you made and how you fixed it.
  • Write what you know first-hand, in plain English and short sentences. Link every rule, deadline or programme to its official source.
  • Do not copy text from other websites, books or guides, even with small changes.
